Assigned scientific question

Potential-field gradient ridges can come from faults, contacts, dikes, intrusions, or acquisition artifacts. The tensor lane tests whether a source is approximately two-dimensional and whether its eigenvector-derived strike agrees with the candidate ridge's own orientation.

Layers

RTP magnetic anomaly and isostatic gravity anomaly, plus official acquisition-block and flight-line metadata for processing masks.

Mechanism

Low-pass first; derive the tensor by FFT within each block; estimate dimensionality and strike; down-weight compact 3-D signatures.

Falsifiable test

On whole held-out fault segments, tensor strike should agree with segment strike more often than matched random ridges do.

Main confounders

Lithologic contacts, intrusions, vents, east–west flight-line striping, block seams, and spectral edge artifacts.
