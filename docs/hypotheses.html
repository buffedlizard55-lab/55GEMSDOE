Same two potential-field layers and block masks; test stability of dimensionality and axial strike over preregistered low-pass scales.		Persistent orientation may distinguish geological structure from derivative noise, short-wavelength compact sources, or survey seams.A stability gate beyond a one-scale ridge or basic cross-gradient. Cost: medium/high. No score estimate.
3Gravity-only near-2-D tensor ridges.
Isostatic gravity anomaly; tensor index, quasi-2-D eigenvector strike, and ridge/strike agreement.		Density boundaries and basin/basement offsets may show a fault continuation that has a weak magnetic response.Distinct from simple gravity-gradient magnitude only if it uses full tensor dimensionality and strike; check archived raster/code. Cost: medium.
4RTP-to-pseudogravity tensor.
Reduced-to-pole magnetics transformed to pseudogravity under an explicit magnetization assumption, then tensor dimensionality and strike.		May make elongated magnetic-source geometry more interpretable as a structural strike where a fault is absent from the catalogue.Different from raw magnetic ridges, but sensitive to magnetization assumptions and prior `DILCOND` work. Cost: medium/high.
