No project GeoTIFF is currently cleared for use. A historical raster remains in the repository for audit traceability, but it is intentionally not linked as a download. Why there is no download button

  • Uniqueness: stop. Separate historical reports record raw 3-pixel overlap of 84.13% (H55 40k), 84.01% (H55 160k), and up to 100% (H56 40k), each above the literal 70% limit. They are candidate-specific historical scans, not a fresh registry scan. The earlier control-adjusted exception is not allowed; the local raster registry and continuous score surface are absent, so uniqueness cannot be rechecked here.
  • Scientific evidence: not cleared. Stored holdout folds can split connected mapped faults; the nominal random arm was conditioned on the candidate score; its interval was bootstrapped over fold scores, not pooled metric contributions. No new experiment was run.
  • Format: partially inspected only. Historical metadata were inspected, but the authentic organizer template/footprint is absent in this checkout and the validation script was not rerun. No organizer validator receipt exists.
  • Score attribution: not confirmed. No organizer receipt ties the reported 0.2778 value to a raster hash.

What a valid organizer submission requires

When every gate has passed, a human competitor must sign in to the DOE GEMS Prize Challenge on DrivenData, open the submission page, upload the exact cleared single-band GeoTIFF, enter the run-card note (at most 140 characters), submit, and preserve the organizer receipt. This repository does not hold portal credentials and has not submitted anything.

Before any download link is published, the project requires: authorized input provenance; a same-evaluator whole-component holdout win with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI; per-feature leakage canaries; pre-placement surface uniqueness; final-dot uniqueness; and format validation against the authentic organizer template. See the machine-readable run card.
